## Caption/prose changes for the manuscript editor

- Replace every analytical `bar b`/effective-scalar threshold with the common
  threshold `sigma^2/(beta*a*C0)` for row-stochastic B.
- State explicitly that crossing `lambda_star` amplifies the common mode only.
  For two symmetric communities, the relative multiplier is
  `rho_minus=rho_plus(1-2 Gamma)`.
- Remove “any lead is amplified.” Use the classifications in
  `results/flowfield_modes.csv`.
- Replace `C_i/sum_j C_j` as a model law with baseline exposure `W_ij C_j,t`.
- Describe retrospective `delta_soc` without confidence weights; confidence
  enters through `g(Cbar)`.
- Use “stochastically generated early lead” or “neutral-initialisation
  stochastic amplification,” not “endogenous early lead.”
